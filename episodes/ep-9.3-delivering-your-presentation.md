## Think Like a Researcher

Aisha's slides are done, and her storyboard has been through three drafts. Then an email arrives from the seminar organiser: 
*"Good news: we'll run this as a hybrid session. Some students will join online. You'll have ten minutes, followed by five minutes of questions."*

Aisha reads it twice. Her slides are ready. But *is she*?

She pictures a student at the back of the room asking a question that nobody online can hear. She pictures a camera, and she isn't sure where to look. 
And, if she's honest, she pictures someone asking something about her study that she simply doesn't know the answer to.

Having good slides and being ready to present are two different things. Let's try to close that gap.

## A Wise Researcher Once Said…

_In his guide to preparing scientific presentations, Daniel Günther names "authenticity and individuality" as two of the most important ingredients of a successful presentation._

That's good news if you're nervous about "performing". You are not being asked to become a different person on stage.
