:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: questions 

- Once a study is finished, what does it mean for it to be genuinely open to the public?
- How is openness relevant to teaching, peer review, collaboration, and software, not just data?
- How do these practices work together in a single project?

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: objectives

Learners will be able to:

- Distinguish open access from preprints, and explain when each applies.
- Describe what open evaluation changes about peer review.
- Explain how team science and open source software extend open science principles beyond data and papers.
- Identify which of these practices best fits a given research scenario.
